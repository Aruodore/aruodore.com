# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, machine-learning engineers, creative technologists, science publications, AI labs, and quantitative teams. They visit to understand the mathematics, inspect a working interactive, and judge the rigor of the practice behind it.

## Product Purpose

Aruodore is a long-term public practice in probability, statistics, computer vision, and machine learning. It combines a quiet gallery of browser-native pieces with a mathematical notebook. Success means a visitor can see a probabilistic idea move, understand the governing mathematics, and trace the claims to sources and code.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, and inquisitive. The voice is direct and technically serious without becoming academic theatre. The interface should feel like a carefully prepared scientific figure rather than a portfolio template.

## Anti-references

- Generic minimal portfolios and editorial templates.
- Marketing funnels, service menus, testimonials, newsletters, and creator-economy patterns.
- Decorative motion, glass effects, rounded card grids, and signal colors used without mathematical meaning.
- Generated-sounding copy built from slogans, broad claims, or repetitive contrast formulas.

## Design Principles

- Show the mathematics through working visual evidence.
- Let pieces carry the visual weight; keep site chrome quiet.
- Make every claim, equation, control, and reference legible and accountable.
- Preserve signal-color meaning across every figure.
- Prefer a small complete artifact over a large unfinished feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. All navigation and controls must work from a keyboard, text must meet contrast requirements, equations must include MathML, and every moving or WebGL figure must provide reduced-motion behavior and a meaningful static fallback.
