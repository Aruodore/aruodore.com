Deep interactives that are planned but not yet built, in recommended order.

Gibbs Sampling shipped on 2026-09-13. The next committed sequence is:

1. **The bootstrap.** A resampling counterpart to the Bayesian pieces, and the natural place to
   put sampling distributions next to posterior distributions.
2. **Gaussian process regression.** The first piece where the posterior is over functions rather
   than parameters.
3. **Kalman filtering.** Sequential Gaussian conditioning applied to a hidden moving object, and
   the first direct bridge into tracking and computer vision.
